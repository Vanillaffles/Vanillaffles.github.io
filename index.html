Expected graduation: June 2027

I am Gang Xie (谢刚), a master’s student in Computer Science at Hangzhou Dianzi University, expected to graduate in June 2027. My research focuses on brain-inspired multimodal affective computing, with an emphasis on robust multimodal emotion and sentiment understanding.

I also work on reliable AI systems for content safety, including multimodal safety evaluation, intent-aware harmful-content detection, and LLM-based prompt optimization. My recent work connects cognitive signals, semantic reasoning, and deployable machine-learning systems.

Research interests

  • Brain-inspired multimodal affective computing
  • Multimodal emotion recognition and sentiment analysis
  • Robustness, signal-noise separation, and cross-dataset generalization
  • LLM safety, prompt optimization, and multimodal content moderation

For research inquiries, please contact me at siegang@163.com. You can also find my work on Google Scholar, OpenReview, GitHub, and ORCID.

selected publications

  1. IntentSafe-CN: A Multi-Layer Diagnostic Benchmark for Intent-Aware Chinese Harmful Content Detection
    Gang Xie and others
    In Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 2027
    Under review; co-first author
    Abs

    A multi-layer diagnostic benchmark that makes surface-form variants, targets, emotions, and communicative intent observable for Chinese content-safety evaluation.

  2. From Enumeration to Principle: Robust Prompt Optimization via Semantic Reflection and Multi-Objective Evolution
    Gang Xie and others
    In Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EMNLP 2026, 2026
    Accepted; first author
    Abs HTML

    PROSE uses semantic reflection, multi-objective evolution, and ensemble-based selection to reduce shortcut learning in prompt optimization for adversarial tasks.

  3. HPC-Net: A Brain-Inspired Isolation-Integration Framework for Robust Multi-Dataset Multimodal Emotion Recognition
    Gang Xie and others
    In Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 2027
    Under review; first author
    Abs

    HPC-Net separates auxiliary supervision from target optimization and uses target-conditioned integration to transfer useful representations across heterogeneous affective-computing datasets.

  4. NeuroSIFT: A Biologically-Inspired Framework with Explicit Signal-Noise Separation for Robust Multimodal Emotion Recognition
    Gang Xie and others
    In 2026 IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing, 2026
    Accepted; first author
    Abs HTML

    NeuroSIFT explicitly separates emotional signals from structured noise with biologically inspired selective inhibition and dual-stream adversarial training.

  5. Cognition-driven Adaptive Semantic Decoding Framework for Multimodal Sentiment Analysis
    Gang Xie and others
    ACM Transactions on Multimedia Computing, Communications, and Applications, 2026
    Accepted; student first author
    Abs HTML

  6. Cognition-Guided Coupled Learning for Camouflaged Object Detection
    Gang Xie and others
    IEEE Transactions on Human-Machine Systems, 2026
    Accepted; student second author
    Abs HTML
